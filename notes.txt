ANALOG SENSORS
	For ADC (ADS1115):
        -   SDA: GPIO 2 (pin 3, parallel with BME280)
        -   SCL: GPIO 3 (pin 5, parallel with BME280))
        -   ADDR: Ground
        -   VDD: 3.3V
        -   GND: Ground
        -   A0: Soil moisture sensor AOUT
		-	A1: Light sensor AOUT

	For Temp/Humidity (BME280):
		-	VCC: 3.3V
		-	GND: Ground
		-	SDA: GPIO 2 (pin 3, parallel with ADS1115)
		-	SCL: GPIO 3 (pin 5, parallel with ADS1115)

DIGITAL SENSORS
	For water level sensors:
		-	Connect OUT pins with GPIO17 (pin 11)
								  GPIO27 (pin 13)
								  GPIO22 (pin 15)
		-	VCC: 3.3V
		-	GND: Ground
